Tip: drag the blue handles to set start / end, drag inside the highlight to move the whole selection, or drag on an empty area to draw a new one.
Output format: the compressed button uses your browser's encoder — Opus (.webm) in Chrome/Firefox/Edge, AAC (.m4a) in Safari. Both play in any modern player. The WAV button is lossless but uncompressed (expect ~10 MB per minute).
